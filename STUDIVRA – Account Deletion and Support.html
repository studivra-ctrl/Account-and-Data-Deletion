STUDIVRA Account and Data Deletion

Understand. Practice. Remember.

Your privacy matters to us. If you no longer wish to use STUDIVRA, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data using the instructions below. You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion.

Request Account Deletion

Deletion Process

  1. 1

    Submit your request

    Enter the Google account email address associated with your STUDIVRA account and submit the request.

  2. 2

    Verify account ownership

    We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing deletion.

  3. 3

    Deletion and confirmation

    After verification, we will process your deletion request and notify you when the process is complete.

What may be deleted

Depending on what STUDIVRA actually stores, deletion may include:

  • Account profile information.
  • Saved notes and learning materials.
  • Flashcards and quiz history.
  • Learning progress and study plans.
  • Associated uploaded files and other account-linked content.

Data retention

Some information may be retained where required by applicable law or where a legitimate retention requirement applies. Any retained information is limited to the applicable purpose and retention period. Copies in backups or third-party systems may remain for a limited time.
